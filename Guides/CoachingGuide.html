Coaching Guide

How a logged session turns into the suggestion card at the top of an exercise's progress screen: who decides, who writes the words, and what happens when the language model can't.

Built: 7 rules Built: card written live by the on-device model Later: Analysis screen on the cloud model

The big picture

Swift decides. The model only talks.

The advice never comes from the language model. Plain Swift rules read your history and pick one suggestion. The model's only job is to put that decision into a title and one sentence.

Hard rules

What must stay true

  • The model never decides. It gets a decision that is already made. A wrong word is annoying; wrong advice is harmful, so advice stays in code that can be read and checked.
  • The card is never empty and never wrong about the advice. No model, a refusal or a broken answer all end with the hand-written text for the same suggestion.
  • Screens never touch the model. They ask CoachAI for words. Which model, which instructions and what happens on failure live in one place.
  • One model session per card, then thrown away. A session remembers what was said in it, so a shared one would let the last card's wording leak into the next and would fill the small on-device memory.
  • A failed set only means "missed". The rules never read the reps on a failed set, because people often mark a set failed without changing the number.
  • No suggestion before three sessions. Two sessions aren't enough to see a pattern.
  • Rest is not measured. The card may say "try a longer rest", but the app doesn't time it. That belongs to people more serious than FitTap's user.

The rules

Seven rules, asked in this order

"Clean" means every set was finished. The words shown are the hand-written fallback; the model writes its own version of the same advice.

WhenWhat the card says
1The latest session had missed sets.
Half or fewer missed, or more than half.		Stay at this weight.
Few missed: finish them all first, try a longer rest. Most missed: stay until you finish every set, or go down a step for a session or two.
2Every set done, but rated hard.Done, but it was hard.
Repeat until it feels medium, or add weight if you feel ready.
3Every set done at a heavier weight than last time.New weight achieved.
Hold it for one or two more sessions before moving up.
4Every set done at a weight where sets were missed last time.You got all your sets.
Stay one or two more sessions and let your body get used to it.
5Three clean sessions at the same weight, reps rising each time (4×8, 4×9, 4×10).Try heavier.
Your reps keep going up. Go up a step.
6Two clean sessions in a row, same weight and reps, at your top reps.Time to add weight.
Go up one step; fewer reps is fine.
7Two clean sessions in a row, same weight and reps, below your top reps.Time to add reps.
Try one more rep per set.

Rules 6 and 7 wait for two sessions on purpose. That is the "2-for-2" rule coaches use: one good session can be a good day, two is a pattern. The full reasoning and the research behind each rule are in FitTap/Coaching/CoachingRules.md.

Reps or weight

How the coach picks between them

It compares your reps now with your top reps: the most reps per set you finished cleanly in the last ten sessions of that exercise. Below your top reps, add reps. At your top reps, add weight. The app never sets a rep target itself; it learns your range from what you log.

Two sessions in a row ofTop repsSuggestion
20 kg, 4×88Add weight
22.5 kg, 4×68, from the 20 kg sessionsAdd reps
22.5 kg, 4×78Add reps
22.5 kg, 4×88Add weight

Someone who always trains 4×8 will only ever be told to add weight, because 8 is the only number the app has seen.

The words

What the model is given, and what it writes

The model gets three short lines. The first becomes the title, the second becomes the message, and the third is the reason the message may end with.

Goes in

Action: Hold 32.5 kg
Detail: Give it one or two more sessions before moving up.
Why: One full session shows it can be done, not that it is settled.

Comes out

Title: Hold 32.5 kg
Message: Give it one or two more sessions before moving up, since one full session shows it can be done.

This shape was reached by testing all eight suggestions many times. Each choice below fixed a fault that showed up in those tests.

Title and message arrive as separate lines

Given one whole sentence, the model split it wrongly: it merged two options into one, or put the advice in the wrong half.

The model sees no session history

With sets and reps in front of it, it made up numbers ("do 4 sets of 2"). Now the weight is the only number it gets.

The example is about running

With a lifting example, the model copied the example's sentence word for word into real cards.

"Write numbers as digits"

Without that line the title came out as "Stay at twenty twopointfive".

The title length is forced, not asked for

A pattern on the title allows two to four words, so "for one or two sessions" has to go in the message.

One reason is worded oddly on purpose

"Finished for the first time at this weight" was refused by the model's safety filter every time. "The first session with every set finished" passes.

What the model adds today is small: it joins the detail and the reason into one sentence and varies the wording a little. Messages can still run past twenty words or miss a full stop.

On screen

How the card builds in front of you

Stats tab opens
CoachAI loads the model ahead of time, so the first words of a card come sooner.
Progress screen opens
History and personal best appear at once. The rules pick the suggestion straight away.
About two seconds
The card appears with the first words of its title, then the message grows a few words at a time. New words fade in and the content below slides down.
If it goes wrong
No model on the device, a refusal, or a broken answer: the card shows the hand-written text. Leaving the screen stops the model.
If there's no model
A small gray line sits above the card, such as "Turn on Apple Intelligence for suggestions written for you. More info". A tap opens an alert that says what to do, with a Settings button. An iPhone that can't run Apple Intelligence gets the line and an alert with only "OK".

Nothing is saved. The model writes the card again on every visit, so the wording can differ from one visit to the next. That was a deliberate choice, to see the card being written.

Changing the wording

Test a prompt change without Xcode

Edit the lines in SuggestionPrompt.swift, then run this from the FitTap folder. It writes all eight suggestions and prints them together. Add numbers to run only some, such as 0 5.

python3 Scripts/suggestion-wording/run.py
  • Run it three or four times. The model's answers change between runs, and a fault may show up one time in four.
  • The script reads the Swift file, so there is nothing to copy across. The one exception is schema.json next to it, which mirrors SuggestionCopy.swift.
  • The Xcode playground runs the same cases, but it crashes when the model refuses one. That crash is inside Apple's framework; use the script.
