# Seed history: May 4 – Nov 2, 2026 (temporary)

Your last six months as the app imports them, once, on the next debug launch — replacing any earlier import. To change something, edit this file and tell me; I copy it into the import.

**How to read and edit a line**

```
Fri May 22 |  17.5 | 10 10 10 10 | medium
   date    | weight | reps of each set | how it felt
```

- **Weight:** the number you enter in the app, in the unit under each exercise's name. `BW` = bodyweight, `+5` = bodyweight plus 5 kg.
- **Reps:** one number per set. **`x`** after a set = failed. **Holds:** `60s` per set.
- **How it felt:** `easy`, `medium` or `hard` — the effort footer. A session with a failed set has none (the footer doesn't show after a failed set).
- **Runs:** `duration | km | avg speed | HR` (speed is for checking; the import works it out). **MMA:** `duration | HR`.
- `---` lines just mark the phases.

**Timeline**

| Phase | Dates | Training |
|---|---|---|
| Base | May 4 – Jul 12 | 4 × 10, light. Mon / Wed / Fri lifting, Sat run |
| Strength | Jul 13 – Aug 23 | 4 × 6, heaviest. Same days |
| MMA month | Aug 24 – Sep 20 | MMA Tue + Thu, lifting Mon + Fri at strength weights, no runs |
| Rest | Sep 21 – Oct 11 | Nothing |
| Return | Oct 12 – Nov 2 | 4 × 8. Mon Limbs, Wed / Fri lifting, Sat run |

Upper days rotate Chest → Back → Shoulders; Limbs is most Mondays, skipped about one week in four before the break. That evens out to 18 Limbs, 16 Chest, 16 Back and 16 Shoulder days. Lifting sessions last 70–75 minutes, runs and MMA about an hour. Last session: Limbs, Mon Nov 2. Nov 4's Back Day is your own real log and isn't part of this file. **How weights move:** a new weight's first session fails a set or two (no effort). Then it climbs — hard, medium, medium, easy, or just medium, easy — and after an easy the weight goes up 2.5 kg. Where a phase has too few sessions, the cycle shortens; a few machine exercises stop one step short of the target.
